This chapter of the IMF's Technical Assistance Handbook explains the statistical methods behind liquidity forecasting for central bank staff with a quantitative background. It is the companion to *Part I: The Institutional Arrangements*, and documents the forecasting framework that IMF teams deliver to central banks through technical assistance.
