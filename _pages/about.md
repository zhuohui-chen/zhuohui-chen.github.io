I am Zhuohui Chen, a second-year PhD student at the [College of Information, University of Maryland, College Park](https://ischool.umd.edu/), where I am advised by Prof. Zubin Jelveh. My research focuses on AI alignment, multilingual AI, and Large Laguage Model for Time Series.

Before starting my PhD at UMD, I worked as a Research Analyst in the [Central Bank Operations Division of the Monetary and Capital Markets Department at the International Monetary Fund](https://www.imf.org/en/home). I have a multidisciplinary background, holding an M.A. in Translation Studies, an M.P.S. in Public Administration, a B.A. in English, and a B.E. in Computer Science.
