Teaching assistant for **Dr. Sarah Lee** (ongoing).

- Lead lab sessions in **R and RStudio**, guiding students through hands-on statistical experiments.
- Lead discussions of core statistical topics.
- Created **The Cookie Series**, interactive browser demos that help students learn and review sampling distributions, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing. All the data are simulated, so students can compare every method against the true answer.
